Questions Brands Ask Before They Check

Is a feature an advert?

No. It’s an article. Sylph’s editors write it about your pieces, your craft and your story. Want an advert as well? Ask on the call.

Who actually gets the magazine?

UHNI and HNI homes on Sylph’s own list, and boardrooms, embassies and estates. 60,000 to 70,000 copies a year, across four editions a month, read by more than 80,000 UHNIs and HNIs. Every edition also features the architects and designers who work on those homes.

How much does it cost?

Every placement has one fixed price, from a quarter page to a full year. You see yours at the end of the check. No haggling, no agent.

What do I need to send?

Photos of your pieces and a few lines about the collection and your brand. No photos yet? Say so in the check.

Do I have to write it?

No. Send the material. Sylph’s editors write it and take it all the way to print.

Can I talk to someone before I pay?

Yes. Smaller placements can be paid online at the end of the check. Bigger packages start with a call with Sylph’s team.

When will it run?

Sylph publishes four editions a month. Once you book, your feature joins the queue for the next edition with room, in the order material arrives. Send it by the middle of a month and it usually runs the month after. Launching a collection or marking an anniversary? Tell us the date and we plan it to the edition.

Does Sylph turn brands down?

Yes. Sylph’s editorial board only features luxury work. If the pieces are right but the photographs are not, the editors ask for a re-shoot or choose the frames that work.

Can Sylph make a book just about us?

Yes. A magazine carries many brands. A coffee table book carries one. Sylph Consultancy’s Legacy Book™ series makes single-brand coffee table books on premium paper and in premium packaging. There are also custom Sylph editions with your brand on the cover, printed for your own clients, like Kathgodown’s 116-page Collector’s Edition. Both are planned on a call.

What is guaranteed?

Publishing. Every page in your package is published.
